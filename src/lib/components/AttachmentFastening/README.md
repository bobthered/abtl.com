# Attachment fastening artwork

Shared SVG content for three physical finishing styles, rendered inside a Sveltewind `Svg` by the Attachments page, option samples, and homepage bento fan. The artwork uses original vector paths; external visual references are not shipped with the site.

- Wired: two strands pass through the tag hole, twist immediately above it, and continue into two free ends.
- Looped: a small hitch passes through the hole; the two unknotted lengths have free ends.
- Knotted: the cord passes through the hole without a knot at the tag; an end knot joins the strands near their free tips.

Coordinates use a hole centered at `(90, 125)` and a drawing area of approximately `180 x 140`. Translate the group to the supplied tag's hole rather than changing its tag geometry. Material variants retain steel/copper colors, white/twine/black cord, and gauge or cord-weight differences. The 26-gauge steel option is thinner than 23- and 21-gauge steel.

String finish selection is owned by the Attachments section and updates the main artwork, material samples, explanatory copy, and enquiry together. These are illustrative construction details, not scale drawings or specified attachment lengths.
