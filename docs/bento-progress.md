The new full-color topic is a separate capability requested by Bob: four-color process (CMYK), up to eight total colors, and 4CP on both face and back. Its one-third-width tile replaces the materials placeholder. Four ink-only CMYK planes float above a tag rotated Y 35, Z -5, X 0 degrees with true perspective and wider layer spacing; hover or keyboard focus rotates the stack toward the viewer and brings the ink into registration. A finite-distance camera makes near ink planes larger and foreshortens their artwork. A localized radial light backdrop in dark mode keeps black ink visible without changing the ink textures. A reusable canvas surface inverse-projects cached texture pixels continuously, avoiding mesh seams and extra WebGL contexts. Transition sampling is capped at 0.75 pixels per CSS pixel and restores up to 1.5x detail when settled; registered artwork bypasses projective sampling. Ink planes have no sheet fills or outlines; the cream background flood was removed from the artwork so unprinted pixels are transparent. The page follows a fictional premium home-fragrance hangtag design study, Orangerie: a citrus botanical illustration on the face and a fragrance story on the back. Ink-channel toggles and registration explain the illustration; the reversible design demonstrates two-sided printing. A project-planning CTA collects artwork, stock/application, quantity, and side requirements. This concept is illustrative, not a customer endorsement or actual fragrance product. Canvas separations are cached; animation runs only during transitions, pauses offscreen or in hidden tabs, and respects reduced motion. The exact supplied tag outline and reinforcement patch SVGs are reused. Artwork is illustrative screen content, not an ICC-managed production proof. Copy describes full-color printing without promising every visible hue, exact screen-to-print matching, extra ink types, or a particular press workflow. Technical reference: [Adobe's process-color overview](https://helpx.adobe.com/indesign/desktop/print/ink-and-color-management/process-colors-cmyk-overview.html).
